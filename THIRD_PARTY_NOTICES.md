# Third-party provenance

This repository retains the study's own project adapters and experimental/plotting code. It does not vendor the upstream Time-IMM/IMM-TSF/TGTSF checkouts, benchmark raw files, pretrained language-model weights or third-party paper PDFs. Upstream identities and source terms are preserved in the scientific records and `DATA_ACCESS.md`.

`scripts/figure_alignment.py` is the local alignment utility used by the research-writing workflow; it is included for figure reconstruction. No blanket relicensing of this utility or upstream material is asserted by this release. Public availability alone is not a replacement for an explicit reuse licence.
